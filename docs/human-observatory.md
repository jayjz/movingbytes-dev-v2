## Audit and three placements

The existing first screen names Jay, but the background that makes him specific
arrives later. The small Observer is detached from the opening composition.
Perspective, principles, Jonah and TEMPER repeatedly use the same left-heading /
right-copy split. Long preambles delay the artwork. The sculpture is credible
but its narrow panel makes it an illustration rather than an encounter.

```text
A / The open notebook       B / The exhibition       C / The human observatory
Jay + field background      Jay / short masthead      Jay / asymmetrical signature
Principles in the margin    Full-width Jonah          Field background + Observer
Jonah / image and essay     Person + principles       Jonah / warm artwork spread
TEMPER / field note         Curiosity / archive       Curiosity / instrument + note
Archive / contact          Contact                   Principles / archive / contact
```

Choose C. The actual background belongs in the opening. Jonah follows the short
personal introduction and perspective, without four principles delaying it.
Curiosity has two entries, with different scales: TEMPER's test-fixture instrument
and the existing field-work observation. Move principles to the archive threshold;
retain every word, anchor and evidence route. The result should read as a person,
an encounter, a question, then inspectable engineering work.

The opening uses an oversized name, offset serif question and a more present
Observer. It is an original typographic composition, not a depiction of Jay's
workspace or handwriting. The Jonah spread uses clay, paper and the complete
marble image; typography and image have their own space. Curiosity returns through
a warm rule to a deep ink surface, with a large question and a precise instrument.
No scroll reveals, fake notebooks, generic astronomy, extra fonts or dependencies.
