About

Field ops is the constraint

Ten years in the field — HVAC, construction, crews that lose signal in basements and job sites. That is the design constraint, not a footnote. I build tools that keep working when the network is gone and the machine is an 8 GB card, not a cluster.

Earlier work (as ForDaCulture) covers contractor data, shop ops, security scanning, and approval queues. Stack: Python, FastAPI, Streamlit, Pydantic, LangGraph, Ollama/llama.cpp, TypeScript, vanilla HTML.
