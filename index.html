Can an investigation be checked independently?

Experimental agent workflow + independent evidence reader · Python

My implementation. I built an investigation workflow that records tool observations and failed execution, plus a separate reader that checks the saved record without importing the producer’s runtime.

Recorded result. The pinned Linux offline replay passes integrity checks for valid captures, reports unavailable scanner output as an error, and rejects altered ledger bytes.

02 / Selected work

Causality before execution.

When is a decision allowed to act?

SHAD0W

A trading simulation can look convincing if it lets a decision act on data before that data was available. SHAD0W makes the timing and permission to act explicit.

My implementation. I separate when a signal becomes available, when it may execute, how simulated actions progress, and whether an independent paper-risk gate permits them.

Evidence. Pinned Python source and regression tests cover same-timestamp rejection, lifecycle transitions, and paper-risk admission. This is source/test inspection; these tests were not rerun for this portfolio update.

Limits. Market-data ingestion and replay are implemented. An observed live session, profitability, and live-capital execution are not established here.

Perspective

Engineering starts outside the demo.

My background is in HVAC and construction: crews, job sites, and work that has to hold up beyond ideal conditions. Hidden state, unclear handoffs, and failures have consequences in field work. That perspective informs how I approach software.

I care about explicit contracts, visible execution state, and failure handling that preserves what happened. My projects make those concerns concrete, with implementation links and explicit verification limits.

How I think

Working principles

Ideas I use to guide decisions and continue to question. Current questions ↓

  1. Intelligence may be probabilistic; authority should be explicit.

    Separate what a system proposes from what it is allowed to do.

  2. Actions should leave inspectable evidence.

    Decisions and outcomes should remain understandable outside the process that produced them.

  3. Failure should preserve useful state.

    Leave enough context to understand what happened and decide what comes next.

  4. Boundaries should preserve meaning.

    Make consequential exchanges explicit without unnecessarily constraining intelligence inside the boundary.
