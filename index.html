CipherLoop.

Experimental framework · tested with mocks

Keep the context. Question the evidence.

CipherLoop separates a tool-heavy investigation into hybrid planning, local tool execution, periodic message cleanup, and an AST evidence check.

At the inspected revision, deterministic graph tests accept a fixture source-to-sink path and reject a constant-input control. Model calls, compression, and file reads are mocked in those tests; live audit behavior remains untested.

03 / Perspective

Engineering starts outside the demo.

My background is in HVAC and construction: crews, job sites, and work that has to hold up beyond ideal conditions. That perspective informs how I approach software, from field workflows to AI systems and security tooling.

I’m interested in systems with understandable boundaries: what runs locally, what depends on a network, and what evidence supports a decision.
